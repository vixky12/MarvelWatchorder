How this archive works

Research notes & source files

Scope: MCU theatrical films and major MCU-connected Sony Spider-Man entries through Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026). Upcoming films are marked separately and are not counted as released entries. This is not a catalogue of every Marvel adaptation (Fox X-Men, older Fantastic Four, animated universes, and standalone Sony spin-offs are separate branches).

Movie poster thumbnails are retrieved dynamically from Wikipedia page images when available; if an image is unavailable, the designed title card remains. IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes links open current external title pages. Descriptions are short spoiler-light summaries, not copied reviews or ratings.

Reference: Disney+ official MCU release-order guide · Rotten Tomatoes MCU chronological guide.
